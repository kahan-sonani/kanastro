# Calculation methods · rules 0.1.0

## Astronomical foundation

- Swiss Ephemeris C 2.10.03 through `@kuntay/swisseph` 0.2.2 (WebAssembly).
- `@kuntay/swisseph-data` 0.2.2: DE441-derived `sepl_18.se1` and `semo_18.se1`, verified against SHA-256 hashes before calculation.
- Geocentric apparent ecliptic positions with Swiss sidereal and speed flags. No topocentric correction.
- Mean node is the engine's analytical mean element. True node uses the engine's osculating element. Ketu is exactly opposite Rahu; node latitude is zero and speed is retained.
- No unannounced fallback to Moshier for physical bodies or the true node.
- The numeric ayanamsa in Calculation details is the apparent value returned by `swe_get_ayanamsa_ex_ut`, including nutation; it is not the mean-only `swe_get_ayanamsa_ut` value.
- Lahiri is sidereal mode 1, not a fixed constant. Alternatives: Raman 3, Krishnamurti 5, True Chitra 27, Yukteshwar 7 and Fagan–Bradley 0.
- Birth civil time is resolved with Temporal and the browser/runtime's IANA time-zone database. Ambiguous DST folds require an explicit earlier/later choice; nonexistent wall-clock times are rejected for every choice. Historical second offsets are preserved where present in the time-zone database.
- The UTC timestamp is used as the UT input to Swiss Ephemeris, with Swiss's standard Delta-T model. No externally supplied DUT1 or historical observational time correction is applied. Runtime time-zone data and uncertain historical birth records limit absolute precision.
- Supported civil birth dates: 1800–2399. Latitude must be strictly between -90 and 90 degrees.
- Rashi and divisional chart house numbers use whole-sign counting from the corresponding Lagna. The North Indian drawing fixes houses and labels their zodiac signs 1–12. It does not represent a separate Bhava Chalit calculation.
- Internal values remain full precision. DMS output truncates to arcseconds; comparisons and segment boundaries use the unrounded number.

Signs in the following table are described using ordinary zodiac names. Subdivision intervals are left-inclusive and right-exclusive. Each division maps the birth longitude directly; higher vargas are not generated by repeatedly dividing a rendered chart.

D30 odd-sign intervals: [0,5) Aries, [5,10) Aquarius, [10,18) Sagittarius, [18,25) Gemini, [25,30) Libra.

D30 even-sign intervals: [0,5) Taurus, [5,12) Virgo, [12,20) Pisces, [20,25) Capricorn, [25,30) Scorpio.

Varga output is a mapped sign. The app does not invent physical planetary degrees for unequal D30 segments. These are selected named conventions; other authors and programs may choose different defaults.

## Nakshatras and panchanga

Twenty-seven equal nakshatras start with Ashwini at sidereal 0 degrees Aries, each spanning 13 degrees 20 minutes, with four padas. Nakshatra lords follow Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury.

Tithi is the Moon-Sun elongation divided into 12-degree intervals; karana uses 6-degree intervals with the fixed beginning/end karanas. Yoga divides the sidereal Sun-plus-Moon longitude into 27 intervals. These are birth-instant elements, not a complete sunrise-based daily calendar; sunrise vara, rise/set times and event end times are not calculated.

## Parashari subset

Classical seven sign rulers are used; Scorpio and Aquarius do not use node co-rulership. Dignity labels are exalted sign, debilitated sign, own sign, or no displayed label. Moolatrikona degrees, friendship classifications and complete strength systems are not implied.

Full graha aspects: all seven classical planets have their seventh; Mars also fourth/eighth, Jupiter fifth/ninth and Saturn third/tenth. Node aspects are excluded. This is sign-based full drishti, not fractional numerical Drik Bala.

Combustion checks the shortest ecliptic-longitude separation from the Sun: Moon 12°, Mars 17°, Mercury 14° direct/12° retrograde, Jupiter 11°, Venus 10° direct/8° retrograde, Saturn 15°. These stated thresholds are a convention, not a universal choice across all texts. No generic yoga, disease, lifespan or event prediction is generated.

## Vimshottari

Moon-nakshatra based. Lord order and years: Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17. The elapsed fraction of the birth nakshatra determines the full mahadasha start, including time before birth; subperiods are computed from that full origin. Two consecutive 120-year cycles are available. Antardasha and pratyantardasha start with their parent lord and are proportional to lord-years / 120.

The default year is 365.25636 days; explicit alternatives are 365.2425 and 360 days. Dates shown in the table use UTC; calculations retain full timestamp precision. Date predictions can differ across programs because of year conventions and birth-time choices.

## Jaimini subset

Seven-karaka order: Atma, Amatya, Bhratri, Matri, Putra, Gnati, Dara, omitting Pitri. Eight-karaka adds Pitri before Putra and includes Rahu, ranked by 30° minus Rahu's degree within its sign. Ketu is always excluded. Rank uses unrounded degrees. Exact numerical ties are explicitly marked unresolved; no secret tie-breaking tradition is claimed.

Rashi drishti: movable signs aspect fixed signs except the adjacent fixed sign; fixed signs aspect movable signs except the adjacent preceding movable sign; dual signs aspect other dual signs.

Arudha: count the source-to-lord sign displacement again from the lord. If the projected pada is in the source sign or its seventh, move that projected sign to its tenth. A1 and A12 are labelled Arudha Lagna and Upapada. Classical seven-lord rulership is used. The Atmakaraka's D9 sign is displayed directly without conflating the different Karakamsha/Swamsha chart presentations.
